Established in 2017, AMR Packwell Private Limited is a leading manufacturer, supplier, and service provider of premium packaging solutions, headquartered in Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India.

We deal exclusively in superior-quality Plywood Boxes, Automatic Strapping Machines, Packing Straps, Corrugated Boxes, and more — all crafted from the finest materials. Every product is rigorously tested across multiple quality parameters before reaching the market, ensuring longevity, ease of use, and unmatched reliability at the best possible prices.

As a trusted service partner, we also fulfill timely requirements for Car Lashing, Wire Rope Tractor Lashing, Box Packaging Services, and more.
