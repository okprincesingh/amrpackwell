About Kraft Paper Edge Protector

The Kraft paper edge protector is a single-sided coated paper that is brown in color and made from high-quality craft paper. It is designed to protect the edges of your products during shipping or handling.

The anti-curl feature ensures that the paper stays in place and doesn't curl up, providing even better protection. The coating on this paper helps to add strength to it, making it a reliable option for all shipping and packaging needs.
