Types of Pine Wooden Pallets

Expert Pine Wooden Pallet Producers produce various kinds of pallets according to different applications. Each business has specific storage and shipping needs, and using the most appropriate type of pallet can provide significant benefits.

1. Two-Way Entry Pine Wooden Pallets

These pallets facilitate loading / unloading from both directions with a forklift. Often found in warehousing environments where goods movement occurs in a fixed direction as an inexpensive means of transport for ordinary logistics.

2. Four-Way Entry Pine Wooden Pallets

Four-way entry pallets allow access from every side. This reduces the loading and unloading time in the factory. It is widely used in many industries to enhance productivity and help handle material swiftly. A four way pallet is suggested by the experts of Pine Wooden Pallet Manufacturers for large-scale distribution centers.

3. Heavy-Duty Industrial Pallets

Designed as pallets to be moved easily while bearing machinery, metal parts or other types of heavy products, these pallets are strengthened so as to be able to support very high weights without losing their shape.

4. Export Pine Wooden Pallets

Export quality Pallets Since the export situation of the product requires the use of general rules of international shipping,export quality pallets are manufactured. The export product manufacturing firms depend on the reliable Pine Wooden Pallet Manufacturers for the export cost saving packaging.

5. Customized Pine Wooden Pallets

Almost all industries need pallets in custom dimensions. Trusted manufacturers manufacture pallets keeping in mind product shape and size, storage system and means of transportation to ensure maximum safety and efficiency.
