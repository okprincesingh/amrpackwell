What Are Wooden Pallets?

A wooden pallet is a flat wooden support structure used for storing, handling, stacking, and transporting goods. Pallets make it easier to move products with forklifts and pallet jacks while reducing the risk of damage during material handling.

High-quality pallets are used across manufacturing, warehousing, retail, pharmaceuticals, automotive, FMCG, and logistics operations. Professional wooden pallet manufacturers design pallets according to load capacity, size, wood type, handling method, and shipment conditions.
