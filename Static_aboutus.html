Company Profile

Who We Are

AMR Packwell Private Limited is a leading manufacturer and supplier of Paper Edge Protectors and premium-quality Wooden Pallets, Wooden Boxes & Crates. Our company was established in 2017 with the aim of providing high-quality, cost-effective, and eco-friendly complete packaging products to various industries.

We specialize in the production of paper edge protectors — also known as corner boards or angle boards — Outer Diameter Protectors, Paper Trays, Paper Pallets, and more, all designed to provide structural support and prevent damage to vulnerable edges and corners of goods. Our paper edge protectors are made using premium-quality kraft paper sourced from reliable and sustainable suppliers.

We have a dedicated unit with sound experience in Wooden Pallets, Wooden Crates, Wood Caging Boxes, Plywood Pallets, and Plywood Boxes. We are also a leading supplier of all types of Corrugated Boxes (3 Ply, 5 Ply), Heavy Duty Boxes, Imported Paper Corrugated Boxes, and Paper Trays through our large-scale manufacturing setup. Our paper edge protectors are 100% recyclable and biodegradable, making them a sustainable alternative to traditional packaging materials.
