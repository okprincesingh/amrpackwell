What Are Wooden Pallets?

A wooden pallet is an open, flat, transport structure that is manufactured from quality wood to provide a firm base to store and move merchandise in. These pallets enable goods to be loaded or unloaded more effectively, by allowing them to be handled with material handling devices such as forklifts. Wooden pallets are capable of holding and carrying great weights, thanks to their sturdy construction.

Top Wooden Pallet manufacturers provide superior quality timber and incorporate cutting-edge manufacturing processes. These pallets come in a range of sizes, load capacities and configurations appropriate to a multitude of industrial requirements.
