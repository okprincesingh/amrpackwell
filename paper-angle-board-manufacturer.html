What is a Paper Angle Board?

Paper angle board, also known as paper corner protector or edge protector is a type of packaging product that has been manufactured by layering multiple layers of recycled kraft paper which are bound together through the strong glues. Paper angle boards can strengthen the edges and the surfaces of the package, protect the package from being crushed during the logistics as well as can improve the carrying capacity.

Expert manufacturers of Paper angle boards produce a large range of thickness, size and length according to different requirements. It's widely used in the logistics, warehousing, manufacturing, and export industries.

What are the advantages of using paper angle boards?

1. Better protection of your goods

Paper angle boards guard the edges and corners of items against impact, crushing, and harm as they are processed or delivered.

2. Greater load security.

By using angle boards in conjunction with pallet stretch wrap or strapping, palletized products are stabilized. Items are also far less likely to shift during transit.

3. Cost-Effective Packaging

Compared to replacing damaged products, investing in quality edge protection is a highly economical solution.

4. Eco-Friendly Material

Paper angle boards are typically manufactured using sustainable resources and are generally recyclable, making them environmentally sound.

5. Increased Stacking Strength

Distributes the pressure across cartons and pallets when loading them.
