Complete Range of Packaging Solutions

AMR Packwell deals in all types of industrial packaging materials. Our premium STRETCH FILMS come in sizes from 6 inches to 24 inches with specialized thickness ranging from 23 micron to 51 micron for maximum safety.

We supply BOPP TAPES in all standard widths (1”, 2”, 3”) with micron options of 34 to 42. For heavy-duty strapping, we provide both PET STRAPS (12MM/15MM) and PP STRAP ROLLS (9MM/11MM) along with high-quality Packing Clips.

Our protective packaging range includes Air Bubble Rolls, Thermocol Sheets, and EPE Foam Rolls, ensuring your products remain damage-free during transit. We also provide Shrink Films for modern packaging needs.
