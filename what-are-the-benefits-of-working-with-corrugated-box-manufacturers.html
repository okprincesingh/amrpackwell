What Are Corrugated Boxes?

A corrugated box is a container made from a specific type of packaging material called corrugated fiberboard. This material is made from an inner and outer layer of paper with a wavy paper layer between them. Because of the wavy paper layer, these boxes are durable and provide cushioning. For these reasons, these boxes are the best option for storage and shipment.

Good Corrugated Box Manufacturers produce enough different type boxes to fit every different industry and product requirement. These boxes are lightweight, cost-effective, and provide a barrier for goods against the elements.

Manufacturing Process of Corrugated Boxes

Leading Manufacturers of Corrugated Boxes utilize a well-defined method to produce consistent quality boxes.

The Selection of Raw materials

First-rate raw materials of kraft paper and liner boards are the foundation of quality boxes. The strength and durability of the boxes are a direct relationship to their quality.

Corrugation

Mediums of paper are fluted by rollers to create the fluting.

Formation of the Board

Once fluted, the paper is sandwiched with liner boards using industrial adhesives to form the corrugated board.

Creating and Shaping

Machinery has advanced to create and shape boards into required designs and dimensions.

Printing and Enhancements

Boxes are customized to have company logos and product information printed. Additional enhancements may be laminations, coatings, or treatments.

Assessment of Quality

Every boxed product is subjected to a quality check to ensure that industry standards are met prior to shipment.
