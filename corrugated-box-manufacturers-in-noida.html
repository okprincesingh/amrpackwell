About Corrugated Boxes

Choose AMR Packwell's premium Corrugated Boxes for all-round industrial, e-commerce, and retail packaging needs. Built with high-grade kraft and corrugated board in 3 Ply, 5 Ply, and 7 Ply configurations, these boxes deliver outstanding compression strength, burst resistance up to 200 kgf/cm², and reliable protection for your products during storage and transit.

Available in A, B, C, E, and F flute types with fully customizable dimensions, our corrugated boxes are engineered for every industry — from fragile electronics and perishable food items to heavy industrial goods and pharmaceutical shipments. Custom printing in flexo, offset, and digital formats ensures your brand identity stands out on every box.

Strength, Versatility & Performance

Corrugated boxes from AMR Packwell perform reliably across warehouses, factory floors, retail shelves, and last-mile delivery networks. Their multi-ply construction offers exceptional stacking strength, while optional moisture-resistant coatings, foam inserts, and internal dividers make them suitable for the most demanding packaging applications. Whether you need die-cut mailer boxes, slotted cartons, or heavy-duty export-grade packaging, we manufacture to your exact specifications.

Eco-Friendly & Certifications

All our corrugated boxes are manufactured using recyclable, FSC-compliant kraft paper, meeting ISO quality standards. We supply to major industrial zones across India via efficient logistics, ensuring consistent quality and on-time delivery for every bulk order dispatched from our facility.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What ply options are available for corrugated boxes?

We offer corrugated boxes in 3 Ply, 5 Ply, and 7 Ply configurations. The right ply depends on your product weight and transit conditions — our team helps you choose the optimal ply for your requirement during the order process.

Q: Can corrugated boxes be custom printed with our brand logo and design?

Yes, absolutely. We offer custom printing using flexographic, offset, and digital printing methods. You can print your brand logo, product information, barcodes, or any artwork on the box surface for a professional, branded look.

Q: What is the minimum order quantity for custom corrugated boxes?

The minimum order quantity is 100 pieces for standard sizes. For fully custom dimensions and printed corrugated boxes, MOQ may vary based on size and specification. Contact our team for exact pricing on your specific requirement.

Q: Are corrugated boxes suitable for food and pharmaceutical packaging?

Yes. Our corrugated boxes can be manufactured using food-grade kraft board and optional moisture-resistant coatings, making them suitable for secondary and tertiary packaging in food, beverage, and pharmaceutical industries.

Q: How quickly can corrugated boxes be delivered across India?

For standard orders, we dispatch within 3 business days to all states across India. For large-volume bulk orders or custom-printed boxes, lead time may be 5–7 business days. We work with reliable logistics partners to ensure on-time delivery.
