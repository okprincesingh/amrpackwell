Frequently Asked
Questions

Everything you need to know about our products, ordering, delivery, and customization.

We manufacture 6 product lines — Paper Edge Protectors, Wooden Pallets, Wooden Boxes & Crates, Paper Core Pipes, Cardboard Trays, and Paper Angle Boards. All products are available in custom sizes and specifications tailored to your industry needs.

Our standard MOQ is 1,000 pieces for most products including Paper Edge Protectors, Angle Boards, and Cardboard Trays. For Wooden Pallets and Crates, MOQ varies based on size and grade. Contact us for exact MOQ details.

Absolutely! 100% of our products are available in custom sizes. For paper products, thickness ranges from 2mm to 7mm and lengths from 50mm to 2000mm. For wooden products, any dimension is possible. Share your specs and we'll quote within 24 hours.

Yes, we deliver PAN India — all states covered. Standard delivery is 1–7 business days depending on location and order volume. We're based in Greater Noida, UP and work with established logistics partners for fast, safe nationwide delivery.

Pricing starts from ₹10–₹30 per piece depending on product type, size, and quantity. We offer competitive bulk discounts — the more you order, the better the per-unit rate. All prices are inclusive of GST. Request a quote for exact pricing.

Our paper products use multi-layer Virgin Kraft paper — glued together under pressure to form strong, rigid structures. We offer Brown and White variants, with optional single-side or double-side coating. All materials are 100% recyclable and eco-friendly.

You can place an order or request a sample by: (1) Filling the inquiry form on our website, (2) Calling us directly, or (3) Emailing us with your product name, dimensions, quantity, and delivery location. We respond within 24 hours with a quote.

Yes. AMR Packwell is GST Registered (GST No: 09AARCA6409K1Z5) and our products comply with quality standards. Every product undergoes multi-level quality testing before dispatch — stress tests, tolerance checks, and material grade verification.

Our manufacturing unit in Greater Noida produces over 5,000 units per day. This ensures we can fulfill large bulk orders without compromising quality or delivery timelines. We have been operating since 2017 with a dedicated team of 20+ professionals.

Yes! We offer Moisture Resistant Paper Angle Boards, Heavy Duty Paper Angle Boards, Glossy Lamination Angle Board Pallets, Moisture Proof Angle Boards, and Corrugated Angle Boards. Specify your requirement in the inquiry and we'll recommend the right product.
