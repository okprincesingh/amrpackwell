What are Stretch Film Suppliers?

Stretch Film Suppliers make and sell stretch wrapping films. These films are used in packaging and securing products. Stretch films are made of linear low-density polyethylene (LLDPE) and are made of highly stretchable plastic film. These films are wrapped around products that need to stay bound and protected.

The primary use of stretch film is to stabilize pallet loads. This means to stabilize the items that are together bound on a pallet and keep them from shifting while being transported. Stretch films keep goods from getting damaged or getting dust or moisture. They keep goods from scratches and other types of damage.

There are multiple types of stretch films. These include

Hand Stretch Film
Machine Stretch Film
Colored Stretch Film
Pre-Stretched Film

Cast Stretch Film

How It Works

Employing elasticity and plasticity, stretch film wraps around pallets and products, and, after it retracts, holds items tightly. This holds items together and hinders movement or shifting, even if they are not secured in place.

The process by which stretch film works is described in the following steps.

01

Products are organized and grouped together on pallets.

02

The stretch film is wrapped around the products by hand or by machine.

03

The film wraps and stretches to tightly bind the load.

04

The film can be wrapped in multiple layers for extra protection.

05

This film resists external force and moisture.

Because the film clings to itself, firm load retention is achieved, and safety is enhanced during transportation.

Different films are used by different industries for varying load weights and sizes. In large manufacturing industries, the use of machine-grade stretch film is typical for automated packing. Small manufacturing industries that utilize manual packing use hand-grade stretch film.

Manufacturing / Working Process

Creating a stretch film requires an advanced manufacturing process.

Step-By-Step Manufacturing Process

1

Selecting Raw Material

In order to manufacture strong stretch films, particular attention is paid to the polyethylene resin selected.

2

Melting

The polyethylene resin is placed in heaters and melted to controllable standards.

3

Extrusion

The melted polyethylene is transferred to extrusion machines to manufacture thin sheets of plastic.

4

Cooling and Stretching

The film is made to stretch beyond its initial size and then cooled. This technique improves film strength and flexibility.

5

Rolling

The film is then rolled according to the preference of the customer.

6

Inspection

Every roll of film is tested to ensure it clings normally, has the right thickness, has the right strength, and meets durability standards.

7

Delivery

After passing all the inspections, the film rolls are made ready for delivery.

In order to manufacture stretch films that meet customer needs, quality is kept high at every manufacturing point.
